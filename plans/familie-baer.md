# Familie Bär: series guide

An original episodic Tipp-Buch series. Each episode is one book (five scenes) about one small everyday
problem in a teddy-bear family that ends warmly. The characters, look and voices stay the same in every
episode, so a new book only needs a new story. Follow `buildbook.md` for everything else.

## Cast

| id | Who | Look (in `BAER`) | Voice (`tools/make_voices.py`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mila` | Mila, 7, the big sister with the ideas | orange dress, red bow, red shoes, `kid` | de-DE-Katja, higher |
| `ben` | Ben, 4, the little brother, always hungry | blue shirt, brown trousers, `kid` | de-DE-Killian, much higher |
| `mama` | Mama Bär | teal top, dark trousers, long sleeves | de-DE-Amala |
| `papa` | Papa Bär | green shirt, blue trousers, glasses | de-DE-Conrad |
| `oma` | Oma Erna, 70, loves her garden, roses and tea | greyer fur, violet cardigan, grey skirt, glasses, pearls, pink flower | de-AT-Ingrid, slower |
| `reh` | Frau Reh, owns the gift and flower shop | green top, cream apron | de-CH-Leni |
| `igel` | Herr Igel, Oma's neighbour | blue-grey shirt, straw hat | de-AT-Jonas, lower |

The animal heads are original designs: a round teddy-bear head (round ears, light muzzle, button eyes, a
stitched seam on the forehead) in honey-brown fur, a deer and a hedgehog. Keep them clearly different from any TV cartoon or brand bear; never copy a
show's look, names or plots.

Sizes in a scene: adults 170–200, Mila about 130–140, Ben about 105–120 (scale down further back in the
picture).

## Episode 1: Omas Geburtstag (A2) — built

| Scene | Day | Place | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| `idee` | Saturday | living room | Dativ: Wem schenken wir was? |
| `laden` | Saturday afternoon | Frau Reh's gift shop | gefallen, gehören, passen + Dativ |
| `backen` | Sunday morning | kitchen | Imperativ, Mengen und Gewichte |
| `ueberraschung` | Sunday 3 pm | Oma's front garden | hinter, vor, neben + Dativ |
| `feier` | Sunday afternoon | Oma's back garden | Perfekt, Glückwünsche |

Facts the pictures must keep: Oma turns 70 on 14 May. The letterbox says "E. Bär". The present is a white cup with red roses
(14 €, 24 € with the flowers). The cake is chocolate, with candles 7 and 0. Ben's paper chain hangs
between two blossoming trees. Oma's front door is green, the family car is blue, and Ben holds three
balloons (red, yellow and blue).

## Ideas for more episodes

- **Ben ist krank:** Kinderärztin, Apotheke, Körper, Termin machen
- **Der erste Tag in der Kita:** Tagesablauf, trennbare Verben
- **Ein Regentag zu Hause:** Spiele, Wetter, Modalverben (an original indoor story)
- **Umzug zu Oma in die Nähe:** Wohnung suchen, Kisten packen
- **Ferien am See:** Reisen, Packliste, Perfekt
